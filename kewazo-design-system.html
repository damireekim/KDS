KEWAZO Design System

Brand foundation, web UI components and customer-facing materials. Body text uses Barlow at 1rem (16px); the LIFTBOT typeface is reserved for the LIFTBOT product wordmark and high-impact display moments.

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space = X (the cap-height of "K"). Always maintain on every side.
Minimum: 60px on screen / 18mm in print.

Co-branding

Shell Bilfinger

When pairing with partners (Shell, Bilfinger, Dow, BrandSafway, Flint Hills, Turner, Bechtel...), use a 1px Mono-300 vertical divider between marks. Keep all logos optically aligned to the same x-height.

Do & Don't

Do

Use the wordmark in Dark 800 or pure white. Maintain clear space and proportions.

Don't

Don't recolour with non-brand hues, distort, skew, outline, or place over busy imagery without scrim.

Typography

Body text and almost every UI element use Barlow. The LIFTBOT typeface is reserved for the LIFTBOT product wordmark and high-impact display moments — never for paragraphs or running copy. Body baseline is 1rem · 16px.
